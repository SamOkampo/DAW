# FLOWDAW Owner Release Decisions

## Product owner

**Samuel Ocampo Echeverry** is the selected individual product owner/licensor of FLOWDAW.

This records product ownership for release-readiness purposes. It does not transfer any third-party rights and does not overclaim contributor-owned material that has not been assigned or otherwise licensed.

## Decision 1 — distribution model

**SELECTED: proprietary/commercial FLOWDAW distribution.**

The public product will use a proprietary FLOWDAW EULA rather than an open-source licence for the main application source. The current draft is `EULA.md`.

FLOW Core is licensed royalty-free for use inside musical/audiovisual productions under `FLOW_CORE_LICENSE.md`, while standalone/sample-pack redistribution remains prohibited.

## Decision 2 — JUCE 9 route

**SELECTED BUSINESS ROUTE: JUCE 9 Starter while eligible.**

The official JUCE 9 EULA currently allows Starter for an individual whose revenue/funding generated by use of the Framework remains within the Starter limit. The repository records the selected route but does not claim contractual acceptance has occurred.

Because the current owner has not yet reached the age required to accept the JUCE EULA independently, the JUCE contractual gate remains pending until either:

- a parent or guardian agrees to the JUCE 9 EULA as required by JUCE's current terms; or
- the owner reaches the required age and completes acceptance independently.

If Starter eligibility is exceeded, the owner must move to an applicable JUCE licence before continuing development/distribution under a route that requires it.

## Decision 3 — signing identities

Provide protected Windows and macOS signing/notarization credentials through the release environment. Never commit private keys, certificate passwords, Apple credentials or tokens.
